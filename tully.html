Windsor's River Run Tully

Tully was born into the Windsor Doodle family on May 25th, 2021. She has since been a wonderful addition to the family here and we are very close to her Guardian home and get to see her regularly. She is loyal and cuddly and just wants to be around her humans. A carbon copy of her mother.

Tully

The Lover




Born May 25th, 2021, Tully has been a joy. She may bully her littermates, but with her humans she is all about the love. Tully is in a Guardian Home in Kingsville, Ontario and she is an absolute joy for her Guardian family, who spoil her rotten. We are very excited to see all the wonderful things she will be able to enjoy and bring into this world. We are expecting her first litter in the summer of 2023. Tully has shown excellent signs of being a smart service dog. She retrieves mail and papers for her family. She stays by her owners side when she is sick or unwell. She is very attentive to feelings and goes where she knows she is needed. Her Guardian home consists of a Mom and a Dad, two boys in the teen years and a Grandmother who spoils Tully rotten. 

Fully tested, Tully's genetic health panel comes back completely clear. Her OFA hips and elbows are fair and negative for dysplasia. Her genetic line can be tracked back to Tegan Park and Rutland Manor, the original breeding programs in Australia for Australian Labradoodles. 

Tully is retired as of 2024 Summer. 

Tully's Certifications

Tully has been tested for genetic health issues common to the Australian Labradoodle with Paw Print Genetics. She is also registered with the Worldwide Australian Labradoodle Association (WALA).
