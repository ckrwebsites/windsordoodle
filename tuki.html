Wow Wow's
Peek-A-Boo

This little red darling is our Tuki. She was born in Humble, Texas at Wow Wow Australian Labradoodles. She is 20lbs of trouble and cuteness - a rampaging beauty. She keeps us all on our toes and we love her very much. She is a miniature Australian Labradoodle and will be producing miniature Australian Labradoodle puppies starting in the year 2026.

Tuki

Tuki

Our Tasmanian Devil




This red thief has stolen her way into our hearts. She has only been with us a short time and already, she has managed to see a great share of North America. She has traveled to Montana, Wyoming, South Dakota, Wisconsin, Alberta, Michigan, Ontario, Texas, Minnesota, Ohio, Pennsylvania and she will be traveling even more this summer to Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Tennessee, Washington DC and Maryland. She has gazed at Heaven's peak on the Going-to-the-Sun road, she has driven along the Mississippi River in two states, she has ventured into Johnston Canyon in Banff and she has climbed the rocks in Tobermory and Flower Pot Island. She has swam in the beaches of Lake Michigan, Lake Erie and Lake Huron. She has walked around Sylvan Lake in the Black Hills of South Dakota and she has taken a whiff of the bison in the beautiful Lamar Valley of Yellowstone National Park. This dog is spoiled. 

She meets and greets every person and she loves to stick her head out the window on slow roads to feel the wind in her face and ears. She loves to be cuddled like a baby and she drives her doggy friends crazy with her rambunctious enthusiasm for play-time. Every day she helps me drop off my children to school and dives out the door to make sure she can come with me to pick them up. Anyone would be lucky to have this wonderful puppy and we are so glad that we may be able to send a piece of her home with you to enjoy with your families in the years to come. 

Expecting her first litter in January 2026
