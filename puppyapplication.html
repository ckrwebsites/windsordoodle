Windsor Doodle Puppy Application

Puppy Application

At Windsor Doodle, we want to know as much about our potential puppy owners as possible in order to place them in the right homes. We ask that you take the time to fill our Puppy Application to begin the process of purchasing or reserving a puppy from Windsor Doodle.

Your Information

Please note that none of your personal information will be shared with any outside sources and your email and phone number will not be given out to any outside companies or advertising agencies. Thank you for your cooperation!

It has come to our attention that hotmail.com, outlook.com, msn.com and live.com emails have begun to send important emails into your junk mail folders. More than just Windsor Doodle, it has begun to even target emails from large, established corporations such as Amazon. If you have one of these emails and wish to receive communication from us via email, we highly suggest that you add "puppies@windsordoodle.com" to your contact list or regularly check your junk mail. We do not spam your email, however the bulk of our communication is done through email. 

Applications Are Paused

Windsor Doodle is on a breeding break. We sent our last litters home in 2026, we are welcoming our own baby in December 2026, and we will be taking 2027 off from breeding — so puppy applications are paused too. We expect breeding to resume in 2028 at the earliest, and applications will reopen then. Thank you so much for your interest and your patience. In the meantime, our past puppies are all over on the Our Puppies page.
