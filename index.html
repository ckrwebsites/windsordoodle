What is an Australian Labradoodle?

First bred as a non-shedding Guide Dog - this particular breed of Labradoodles has been in existence for over 30 years. Originally bred in Australia, their genealogy consists of not only Labrador Retriever and Poodle, but also the Cocker Spaniel. These dogs are bred for their sympathetic and gentle temperament, their superior intelligence and their low to non-shedding qualities. An Australian Labradoodle is a very unique breed of dog that stands out from the rest of the pack. They are easily-trained and very good with children and adults - an excellent companion dog.
