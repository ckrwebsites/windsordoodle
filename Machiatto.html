Windsor Doodle's Macchiato

Meet Macchiato, or as her family likes to call her, Olive. She was born here at Windsor Doodle to Tully and Vincenzo. She is clever and kind and tolerant - a wonderful family dog. She is a medium/moyen Australian Labradoodle and will be producing medium Australian Labradoodle puppies starting in the year 2026.  

PEDIGREE

Olive

Olive

Our Child-Friendly Protector




Olive Stays in a Guardian home in Leamington and has a family of 2 adults and 2 young children, as well as an older doggie brother named Doug. Her family has a new baby on the way. She is amazing with her kids and protects them to the her best capability. Her cleverness knows no bounds - she will table surf when the family isn't looking, but instead of eating all the food, she will take a few bites so that no one notices that food is missing and then hop down from the table before anyone can catch her red-pawed. 

She will try to learn about new people, but at the end of the day, she is extremely loyal to her own family. She loves her big brother, Doug, and she always wants to play. She can usually be found around the mommy of the household, but she spends much of her time with the small children and is very tolerant of their play time. Olive's family adores her and can't remember life before this little whirlwind came along. 

Her testing will be coming this spring and if all goes well, she will be joining our breeding program as one of our new mothers. 

Expecting her first litter in January 2026
